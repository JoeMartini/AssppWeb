fix: retry on all transient Apple auth failures (301/no-location, 404, HTML body)

Apple's servers return 301 (no Location), 404, 403, 204, 500, and HTML
bodies intermittently even with a valid SAP signature. Now ALL non-200
responses, empty bodies, and plist parse failures are retried up to 6
times with 2s delay. Only 2FA requirement and valid plist results
short-circuit the retry loop.
