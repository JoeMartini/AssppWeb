fix: retry on 204/empty auth responses (Apple server-side flakiness)

Apple's auth servers intermittently return 204/empty responses even with
a valid SAP signature. This is a known server-side issue confirmed by
ipatool issue #530 ("after some retries it works well").

Increase max attempts from 2 to 6 and add 2s delay between retries on
empty body responses instead of failing immediately.
